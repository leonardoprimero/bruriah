# STRAT-8421: Defense Motion Strategy in Smith v. Johnson

## 1. Procedural Context
In case 8421/2026 (Commercial Tort / Damages), plaintiff relies primarily on the accounting report quantifying alleged lost profits. Verification at the court registry reveals the expert accountant was suspended from the jurisdictional roll on the filing date.

## 2. Decision Outcome
File an immediate motion for absolute procedural nullity (*incidente de nulidad absoluta*) regarding the accounting report and refuse plaintiff's settlement overtures.

## 3. Evaluated Alternatives
- **Out-of-Court Settlement (Acuerdo Extrajudicial)**: Rejected. Settling now would forfeit our dominant procedural position. Viable if the court ratifies the expert's standing with retroactive effect.
