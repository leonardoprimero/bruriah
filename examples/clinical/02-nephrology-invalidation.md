# LAB-9104: Acute Renal Impairment Panel

## 1. Clinical Context
Follow-up laboratory findings for Patient #4812 show rapid decline in renal function. Serum creatinine increased to 2.8 mg/dL and eGFR dropped to 24 mL/min/1.73m² (Stage 4 Chronic Kidney Disease / acute on chronic).

## 2. Invalidation Notice
The premise `renal-clearance-normal` is formally invalidated. ACE inhibitors (Enalapril) carry high risk of refractory hyperkalemia and further hemodynamic decline in renal perfusion. Prior rejected alternative (Amlodipine) or alternative non-renally cleared vasodilators require immediate clinical reevaluation.
